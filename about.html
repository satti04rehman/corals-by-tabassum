Our Story

Meet Tabassum

The woman behind the pieces you love.

Corals by Tabassum began with a simple love for beautiful everyday jewelry. What started as a small personal collection slowly turned into a page, then into a trusted business where hundreds of women across Pakistan now shop their daily-wear pieces.

Every piece on our page is handpicked with care — for its design, its comfort and its quality. We believe jewelry should be wearable, not just pretty in a box. That's why our pieces are lightweight, skin-friendly and made to go from morning errands to evening dinners.

We're also proud to offer Cash on Delivery across Pakistan, so every woman can shop with confidence — no cards, no fuss, just beautiful jewelry at her doorstep.

— Tabassum
